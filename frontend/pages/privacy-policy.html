PharmaTrack

Pharmacy Management System

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PharmaTrack is a pharmacy inventory and point-of-sale management system developed as an academic thesis project. This page explains what information the system collects, why, and how it is used and protected.

What information is collected

PharmaTrack is used internally by pharmacy staff (Owners, Admins, and Pharmacy Assistants) and collects:

  • Staff account information: name and email address, used for login and for sending one-time verification codes (OTP) for password resets and sensitive account actions.
  • Inventory and sales data: product information, stock levels, and transaction records, entered and used by pharmacy staff in the course of normal operations.
  • Audit logs: a record of significant actions taken within the system (e.g. account changes, voided transactions), used for accountability and troubleshooting.

PharmaTrack does not collect information from customers of the pharmacy directly, and does not sell or share any collected information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

How email is used

PharmaTrack sends transactional emails only — one-time verification codes for actions such as password resets, email address changes, and other sensitive account actions within User Management. These emails are sent via the Gmail API using an authorized system account (pharma.track.ph@gmail.com), and are never used for marketing or shared with any third party.

Data storage and security

Data is stored in a managed MySQL database. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed before storage. One-time verification codes are also hashed and expire automatically after a short window.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the PharmaTrack system can be directed to pharma.track.ph@gmail.com.
